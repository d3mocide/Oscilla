01B / Design philosophy

Field instrument by way of a mystic observatory.

Oscilla draws from retro-futurist technical diagrams, CRT-era reconnaissance interfaces, ASCII compositions, and sacred-geometric line art. The mood is an 80s science-fiction instrument confronting an invisible field: symmetric, luminous, slightly uncanny—but always factual and usable.

Geometry has a job

Orbital paths, hexes, grids and axial symmetry express orientation, relationships, and measured space. They frame information or reveal structure; they are never wallpaper behind a reading task.

Signal before spectacle

The visual language may feel esoteric, but every highlight maps to a real condition: green is ready, yellow is calibrated focus, pink is selected or notable, red is fault. Meaning survives when the flourish is removed.

Intentional lo-fi

Use blocky type, sparse phosphor color, crisp line art and bounded redraws—not faux damage, scanline nostalgia, noise texture, gradients or bloom. It should feel made for the hardware, not styled over it.

Composition rule

Build from a clear center, axis or field boundary. Pair measured asymmetry in live data with an underlying geometric order. Empty black space is part of the instrument, giving anomalies and focal points room to speak.

Hierarchy rule

Truth → action → context → atmosphere. Data freshness and controls come first; selected detail comes second; geometry supplies orientation only after those needs are met.

Device rendering

Solid 16-bit colors only. No gradients, transparency, glow, or hairlines in the shipped display UI. Preserve daylight and lowest-brightness legibility.

Data truth

One shallow deck, focused instrument views.

Primary deck gallery.

Each card strictly honors the four-row budget, keyboard soft keys, and the mandatory ◇ RX-ONLY footer tripwire.

Deck typography & layout contract Implementation target (ST7789V2 240 × 135 @ 2.2× Canvas)
Primary interaction & hero numbers Render at 20–26 physical px (44–58 px at 2.2× scale): active targets, RSSI (−58 dBm), frequency (915 MHz), and key metric counters.
Standard row typography & view labels Render at 10–12 physical px (21–26 px at 2.2× scale): view title, subheads, AP names, channel tags, and telemetry readout.
Micro indicators & badges Render at 8–9 physical px (17–20 px at 2.2× scale): battery status, pill badges ([ACTIVE], [SAVED]), and alert sub-modes.
Chrome budget Top bar 38 px (17 physical px), footer 34 px (15 physical px); remaining 225 px (103 physical px) belongs strictly to view body. Physical soft keys live on the chassis exterior; Oscilla Lens Core mark anchors the footer right.
05 / External display · ILI9341 landscape 320 × 240 @ 2× Canvas

Secondary panel is a clean, crisp data viewport.

The external display is horizontal and intentionally chrome-free: no duplicated top bar, status dots, battery, transport strip, or command footer. Displayed at 2× canvas scale matching the Cardputer ADV console, every pixel is dedicated to high-density tactical visualizers, spatial RF radar, and packet stream dissection.

06 / Engineering handoff

Implement shared rules before individual screens.

LayerMaster ruleImplementation result
Brand Lens Core + “Observe the noise.” only. Keep a complete SVG, a small lens/dot glyph, and the defined safe area. Pink stays out of the mark.
Deck chrome Card/view name upper-left; B/G/S/R dots upper-right; battery always terminal far-right; transport below. Build one shared renderer. LIVE, backpack state, ping/pong and age have stable meanings on every view.
External viewport No top bar, status dots, battery, transport strip, footer, or controls. Render only data visualization and selected-item context. The deck is authoritative for navigation, control, and system truth.
Readability & Rows Large interaction labels; strict 4-row budget; no duplicate body title. Set primary menu and state to 14–16 px, supporting values to 9–10 px, and avoid shrinking data below 8 px.
Truth & Invariants Show source and freshness everywhere. Strict receive-only tripwire. Use live/age/cached strings plus dots; every deck footer carries the ◇ RX-ONLY tag. Zero transmit verbs ([AGENTS.md §3.1](file:///d:/Projects/Oscilla/AGENTS.md)).
Rendering 240×135 primary; 320×240 landscape secondary. Bounded redraws, Canvas on deck, fixed-width data type, and serialized SD/TFT transactions on shared SPI.
