01B / Design philosophy

Field instrument by way of a mystic observatory.

Oscilla draws from retro-futurist technical diagrams, CRT-era reconnaissance interfaces, ASCII compositions, and sacred-geometric line art. The mood is an 80s science-fiction instrument confronting an invisible field: symmetric, luminous, slightly uncanny—but always factual and usable.

Geometry has a job

Orbital paths, hexes, grids and axial symmetry express orientation, relationships, and measured space. They frame information or reveal structure; they are never wallpaper behind a reading task.

Signal before spectacle

The visual language may feel esoteric, but every highlight maps to a real condition: green is ready, yellow is calibrated focus, pink is selected or notable, red is fault. Meaning survives when the flourish is removed.

Intentional lo-fi

Use blocky type, sparse phosphor color, crisp line art and bounded redraws—not faux damage, scanline nostalgia, noise texture, gradients or bloom. It should feel made for the hardware, not styled over it.

Composition rule

Build from a clear center, axis or field boundary. Pair measured asymmetry in live data with an underlying geometric order. Empty black space is part of the instrument, giving anomalies and focal points room to speak.

Hierarchy rule

Truth → action → context → atmosphere. Data freshness and controls come first; selected detail comes second; geometry supplies orientation only after those needs are met.

Device rendering

Solid 16-bit colors only. No gradients, transparency, glow, or hairlines in the shipped display UI. Preserve daylight and lowest-brightness legibility.

Data truth

Every observation declares LIVE, a concrete age, or CACHED. Stored data must never visually impersonate live RF.

04 / Cardputer ADV · 240 × 135

Primary deck gallery.

The card name is now the upper-left title—never “OSC.” The top-right cluster is fixed: Backpack, GNSS, SD and RF dots first; battery is the terminal, conventional far-right icon. The second strip carries live state and backpack telemetry. Each card is limited to large labels and a maximum of four list rows.

Deck typography / layout ruleImplementation target
Primary interaction labelsRender at roughly 14–16 physical px: view name, selected row, primary state, and active command. Do not use this tier for more than four rows.
Supporting valuesRender at roughly 9–10 physical px for RSSI, age, counts, and the transport strip. Use fixed width and stable columns.
Footer / contextRender at 8–9 physical px and keep one short line. It may hint controls but does not carry essential live truth.
Chrome allocationTop bar 20 px, transport 18 px, footer 14 px; the remaining ~83 px belongs to the view. The title is never duplicated in the body.
05 / External display · ILI9341 landscape 320 × 240

Secondary panel is a clean data viewport.

The external display is horizontal and intentionally chrome-free: no duplicated top bar, status dots, battery, transport strip, or command footer. The Cardputer remains the control surface and source of all system status. This panel shows unique analysis, context, and selected-item detail only; it adds nothing required for operating the instrument. Touch and readback are not assumed.
